# Privacy

Hisab Khata stores your ledger on your device. The app itself includes no analytics
or tracking code. Android's automatic app backup is disabled.

When you choose a local backup or export, the selected file is saved or shared where
you direct it. When you choose Google Drive backup, a copy is sent to the private
app-data folder of the Google account you select; it is not sent to a separate Hisab
Khata server. The app stores the selected account name and last backup times locally.
Google authorization and Drive services are subject to Google's own policies.

JSON backups and CSV, Excel or PDF exports are not encrypted by this app. Anyone with
access to those files, your device or your Google account may be able to read them.
Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the local ledger, so keep and test a
separate JSON backup. There is no app lock or automatic scheduled backup in this version.
